Welcome to the Official Website of the Duhabi-4 Saraswati Puja Samiti

Every year, our community comes together with immense devotion and joy to celebrate Saraswati Puja, honoring the Goddess of knowledge, music, art, and wisdom. For years, our Samiti has dedicated itself to organizing this auspicious festival, creating a vibrant space for devotees to gather, pray, and seek blessings for their academic and creative journeys.

This website is your central hub for everything related to our annual celebration. Here, you will find real-time updates on event schedules, cultural programs, and community initiatives.
